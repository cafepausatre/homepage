Concept

A three-minute pause in your busy day.

We’re a small specialty coffee bar in Nishi-Shimbashi, Tokyo. Drop in for a takeaway, or enjoy a cup at our counter.

Inspired by Italy’s espresso bars, we bring good coffee and a little conversation to everyday life. Before work, after lunch, or on your way home — there’s always time for a small pause.

Visiting Tokyo? Exploring Shimbashi or Toranomon? Come on in, even if you’re on your own. Have a question about your trip in Japan? Just ask us.

Quality coffee at everyday prices. Made with care, served without fuss.
