Cafe Pausa Tre Officially Opens in Nishi-Shimbashi

Cafe Pausa Tre officially opened in Nishi-Shimbashi on January 13, 2026.

Built around the idea of “A three-minute pause in your busy day,” Pausa Tre is a small specialty coffee bar inspired by Italy’s everyday espresso-bar culture.

Whether it is before work, between meetings, after lunch or while exploring the neighborhood, we want to be an easy place to stop for a well-made coffee.

If you are in Nishi-Shimbashi, we would be happy to welcome you for a cup.
