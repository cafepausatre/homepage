USAPAN Korokoro Rice-Flour Bread Now Available

From September 7, 2026, Cafe Pausa Tre began serving Korokoro Rice-Flour Bread from USAPAN.

It is a bite-size version of USAPAN’s popular “Kiseki no Komeko Pan” rice-flour bread. USAPAN describes it as made without wheat and with 100% rice flour, with the same memorable chewy texture as the original.

At Pausa Tre, it is available as 3 pieces for ¥290.

Enjoy it with your morning coffee or as a light bite between work.
