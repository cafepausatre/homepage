Coffee Pot Service for Offices & Events

Cafe Pausa Tre offers hot specialty coffee in insulated serving pots for meetings, workshops, office gatherings and events. Enjoy coffee prepared at our shop, ready to share with your team or guests.

Price: ¥3,000 for 10 cups (tax included, with 10 paper cups).

Coffee: Choose Brazilian or Ethiopian beans. Our standard order unit is 10 cups.

Extras: Milk, sugar and stirrers are available for an additional charge.

Pickup & delivery: Collect your order at our shop, or ask about delivery around Nishi-Shimbashi, Shimbashi and Toranomon. We lend the insulated pots free of charge; please return them after use. Delivery availability and return arrangements are confirmed when ordering.

Payment: Credit card or bank transfer by invoice.

To enquire or request a quote, please send us your preferred date and time, number of cups, and pickup or delivery preference by email or Instagram DM.
